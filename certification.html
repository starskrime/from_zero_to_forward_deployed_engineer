How to think on exam day

  • Several answers will look reasonable. Pick the proportionate fix for the root cause, not the biggest change.
  • For critical rules, prefer programmatic enforcement (hooks, gates) over prompt instructions.
  • Before adding a routing layer, check whether better tool descriptions would solve it.
  • Explicit criteria and a few targeted examples beat vague instructions, sentiment, and self-reported confidence.
  • Independent reviewers and focused multi-pass review beat self-review and one giant prompt.
  • Watch for "multiple response" questions: each one tells you how many answers to pick.
