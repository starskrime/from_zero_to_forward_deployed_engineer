    About this program

    FDE Preparation is an open, self-paced program for engineers who want to become enterprise-ready Forward Deployed Engineers: the people who sit with a customer, scope a real problem, and ship a production AI system into their environment. You learn by building ten portfolio projects in different industries, prepare for the Claude Certified Architect – Foundations exam, and study the security, compliance, integration and business skills that enterprise FDE roles ask for.

    Your name and progress are stored only in your browser. Links go to official documentation, original papers, engineering blogs and conference talks; each lesson re-checks its links before it's published.
